# Data provenance

| Dataset | Source | Access | Version |
|---|---|---|---|
| TCGA-BRCA STAR counts | GDC API (open access) | api.gdc.cancer.gov | GENCODE v36 annotation; accessed 2026-09 |
| GTEx v10 breast + adipose (subcutaneous) | public GCS bucket (adult-gtex) | storage.googleapis.com/adult-gtex | GTEx v10, RNASeQC v2.4.2 |
| METABRIC expression + clinical | cBioPortal API, study brca_metabric (raw Illumina HT-12 v3) | cbioportal.org/api | accessed 2026-09 |
| PAM50 centroids | genefu (BHKL lab) official data object ssp2006 | github.com/bhklab/genefu | Parker et al. 2009 rule |
| ESTIMATE gene sets | official R package (SourceForge, estimateproject) | sourceforge.net/projects/estimateproject | Yoshihara et al. 2013 |

## Citation requirements

The results shown here are in whole or part based upon data generated by
the TCGA Research Network: https://www.cancer.gov/tcga

GTEx Consortium (2020), Science 369:1318-1330. METABRIC: Curtis et al.
(2012), Nature 486:346-352, accessed via cBioPortal (Cerami 2012; Gao
2013). ESTIMATE: Yoshihara et al. (2013). PAM50: Parker et al. (2009).
